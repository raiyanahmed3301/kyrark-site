Privacy

This website does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking scripts.

Enquiries

If you submit an enquiry, the name, organisation, work email, selected service, consent, and scope message you provide are sent to Resend for email delivery to Kyrark. The sender address is included as the reply-to address so Kyrark can respond. Do not include passwords, credentials, or other sensitive access data in the form.

Resend processes the message under its own service terms and privacy practices. The site does not store enquiry submissions in a database. Contact Kyrark at cursedblademaster@protonmail.com with privacy questions.

Server logs

The hosting provider may process standard request data, such as an IP address and requested URL, to operate and secure the site. Consult your hosting provider’s privacy information for details about its retention and processing.
